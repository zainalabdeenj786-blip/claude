plus each competitor's own page.*

## How these are ranked (please read)

**Meta does not publish sales, spend or impressions for commercial ads in the UK.** No public tool can see another brand's sales; paid
"spy tools" only estimate them. So this list ranks by the two signals that reliably point to winning ads:

1. **Days running.** Brands switch off ads that lose money, so an ad still live after weeks is profitable.
2. **Scaling.** When a brand launches dozens of copies of one concept at once, it's pouring budget into something that works.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Proven** | Running 3+ weeks: almost certainly profitable |
| 🟡 **Scaling** | Many near-identical variations launched together: a winner being pushed hard |
| ⚪ **Testing** | New or small: worth watching, not yet proven |

## What this tells RizqPure

**1. Free gifts dominate halal bundle offers, and 15% off looks weak next to them.** Sunna (3 free gifts), Noor (40% off + 3 free gifts),
Zooki (25% + free gifts) and Halal Community (buy 2, get 1 free) are all scaling gift-led offers right now. A plain 15% off sits below the market.
→ Add a **free gift to the 3-pack**. You already have a free *"RizqPure Halal Collagen: Maximise Guide"* product on your store, which makes a
ready-made gift. A branded scoop, frother or tote also works. Headline idea: *"Your 3-pack + free gifts."*

**2. Social proof runs longest.** Absolute Collagen's longest-running ads are expert endorsement, celebrity endorsement and real customer
reviews, not discounts. → Your strongest long-term creative will be **real customer photos or videos and quotes from batch 1** (with permission).

**3. Anti-ageing is the evergreen hook.** "Age Powerfully" has run for six weeks. → Keep the younger-looking skin and fine-lines angle at
the front, as in your current ads.

**4. Listicles and call-outs are being tested hard.** "8 Signs Your Body Needs Collagen" and "Read if you're tired of looking older than you feel"
match the format of your `5-signs` and `this-is-for-you-if` ads, so those formats suit this market.

**5. Watch Luma.** A new *women's halal collagen* brand started advertising today, aimed at exactly your audience. Expect the halal space to get busier.

**6. "Calling" works.** Sunna's "FREE Gifts Calling!" uses the same idea as your "…is calling" ads, so a gift version of yours is a natural next test.

## Want real sales and impression numbers?
Only available to the advertiser themselves. Paid tools (e.g. Foreplay, Minea, PiPiADS, AdSpy) **estimate** reach and spend. Useful for
direction, but not true sales data. The days-running method above is what most performance marketers use for free.
