# RizqPure: one page

**The customer.** UK Muslims aged 22–55 who care about halal and shop online, especially household decision-makers, people into beauty, strict halal checkers and gym-goers. They trust certifiers they recognise, Muslim creators and family WhatsApp groups. They don't trust brand-hosted reviews.

**The offer and price.** RizqPure Halal Collagen: 10g of grass-fed bovine collagen peptides a day, with vitamin C and biotin, in a 300g pouch that lasts 30 days.
- £18 for one pouch, £32 for two, £45 for three, or £15.30 a month on subscription (free delivery, a reminder before every charge, one-click cancel). Today: 2-pack £30, subscription £14.40.
- A £4.95 seven-day trial, credited to the first pouch.
- A 60-day money-back guarantee.
- The certifier and certificate number on the pouch, and lab results published for every batch.
- Every pouch funds meals through a named partner.
