## What would flip a no

- My sisters or cousins in the WhatsApp group trying it and saying it really helped their joints or hair, and the certifier being one we already trust. Then I might try the £4.95 pack.
- Someone in the family WhatsApp group saying they'd used it for their joints and it helped, plus seeing it on Amazon with reviews so I can grab it when it's discounted.
- Finding out for sure my current collagen isn't halal, or seeing it on Amazon at around £13-14 for the pouch so I can add it to my usual order.
- Links to proper independent studies showing oral collagen at 10g a day makes a measurable difference to skin or joints. If I saw that, I'd try the £4.95 trial.
- Seeing proper evidence it helps joint recovery, or a recommendation from someone at my gym who's noticed a real difference, so it feels like a need rather than a treat.
- A couple of people in my family WhatsApp group using it for a month or two and saying their joints actually feel better, plus seeing the certifier is one I trust, like HMC.
- Published results from a proper independent trial on this product or this dose showing a measurable improvement in skin or joints, not just purity testing.
- Proper human trial results for this dose of collagen showing a visible difference in joints or skin within a couple of months, linked from the product page.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
