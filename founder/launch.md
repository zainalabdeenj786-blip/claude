## Step 1: test before you spend (Oct 12 – Nov 1)

The cheapest real test: **a pre-order list for the trial pack, using a page and the founder's own channels only, with no paid ads.**

- Put up a page: "The 7-Day Halal Collagen Trial, £4.95. Certified by [BODY], certificate here. Join the list for first dispatch on Nov 23."
- Better if Shopify allows it: **take real £4.95 pre-orders** (paid beats a waitlist).
- Send it to existing customers (email), your social accounts and 3-5 community WhatsApp groups you're already in.

**Success line, written before running it:** **150 paid trial pre-orders in 3 weeks**, or 300 waitlist sign-ups, from organic channels only. Also ask every existing customer one question: "Before buying, did you check who certified us? Would seeing it on the pack have made you buy sooner?"

Compare with the panel: the panel said 60% "would try". If real numbers fall well short of the success line, trust the real buyers, and go back to `offer.md` (is the certifier one this audience accepts?) before printing anything.
