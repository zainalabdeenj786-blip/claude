# RizqPure: "Only 1% can pause at the right time" (pause challenge)

## Why the original got 13.4M views
- **It's a game.** A red outline is fixed on screen. The skater (wearing the drop) only shows up for **one or two frames at a time**, flickering in and out. People pause over and over to land him inside the outline.
- **Rewatching is the whole point.** The clip is 6 seconds and loops. Every failed pause is another view, and the algorithm pays out for watch time.
- **The product fills the outline.** The frame everyone is trying to catch is a close-up of the hoodie, so viewers stare at it on purpose.
- **Comments come built in.** People post "got it 😤", "impossible", or screenshots. The fake "ALMOST HAD IT, 2 frames off" sticker adds to that.
- **It looks natural.** Phone footage on a sunny path, nobody acting.

## RizqPure version: "Catch the pouch"
The red outline is the **RizqPure pouch**. The viewer wins when the real pouch lands exactly inside it.

### Shot (pick one, all shot on a phone at eye level, 6 s)
1. **The toss (recommended).** Phone on a tripod in a sunny kitchen or park. Someone stands about 3 m back and **tosses the pouch** in a slow arc toward the camera, or a friend throws it in from off-screen and the person catches it right where the outline is. Film it at 60 fps.
2. **The walk-up.** Someone walks toward the camera holding the pouch at chest height, front label facing out, and stops with it held up to the lens. This one is closest to the original.
3. **The gym bag.** Someone pulls the pouch out of a gym bag and swings it up to the camera.

**Rules:** the label faces the camera at the "catch" moment. Use good daylight so the gold logo shines. Keep the background calm so the navy pouch stands out.

### Edit (CapCut, about 15 min)
1. Import the clip at 1080×1920. Trim it to about 6 s that end on the frame where the pouch is biggest, sharpest and facing the camera. Call that the **catch frame**.
2. **Flicker:** split the clip so the pouch only shows for **1–2 frames** at a time, 4–6 times through the video. Fill the gaps with a frame of the empty background (freeze a frame from before the pouch enters). The real catch frame goes in **once**, about 2/3 of the way through.
3. Add `03_overlay_combined.png` as an overlay across the whole clip. Scale and move it so the red outline sits **exactly** on the pouch in the catch frame. You can also use `01_pouch_outline.png` and `02_hook_text.png` separately.
4. Add `04_almost_had_it_sticker.png` on **one** near-miss frame, where the pouch is a little off the outline.
5. Use a trending low-key sound with no voiceover. Turn looping on, and don't add an end card because it breaks the loop.
